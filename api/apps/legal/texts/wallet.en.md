The Kuulis wallet exists to pay the driver's monthly subscription.

## 1. What the balance is
- It is **prepaid credit** to use within Kuulis, in USD. It is not a bank account, earns no interest and is
  not electronic money.
- **It cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money.** Kuulis does not refund the balance, except for an error
  on our side.

## 2. Top-ups
- You top up with USDT through Binance Pay to the Kuulis account the app shows, with the minimum amount shown.
- Register your Binance Pay ID in the app so your top-ups are credited automatically. If it does not match,
  the team reviews and assigns it by hand.
- Binance or network fees, if any, are paid by whoever tops up.

## 3. Using the balance
- The monthly fee is taken from the balance automatically.
- Kuulis credits here the difference on promotional rides and other benefits, such as inviting other drivers.

## 4. Transfers
You can transfer balance to another driver, with a monthly limit on what you send. Check the recipient
carefully: a transfer cannot be undone.

## 5. Adjustments
The Kuulis team may correct the balance for errors, fraud or claims, with a recorded reason. Large adjustments
are reviewed by a second person.

## 6. If you delete your account
**The balance is lost** when you delete your account. Transfer it first if you want to keep it.
