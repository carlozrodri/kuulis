## 1. Who we are and what Kuulis does
Kuulis is an app that **connects** people who need a ride (riders) with independent motorbike or car drivers
who offer that service (drivers). Kuulis is operated by [Name or company, RIF and address] ("Kuulis", "we").

**Kuulis does not provide transportation.** A ride is an agreement between the rider and the driver. Kuulis
provides the technology to meet, price the ride, follow it and report problems.

## 2. Acceptance
By creating an account or using the app you accept these Terms, the Privacy policy and the Community
guidelines. If you do not agree, do not use Kuulis. When we make an important change we will show it in the app
and you will need to accept it to keep requesting rides.

## 3. Account requirements
- You must be **over 18**. A minor may only ride together with an adult who has their own account.
- Your details must be real and up to date. The account is personal: do not share or sell it.
- You are responsible for what happens with your account. If you think someone accessed it, change your
  password and contact us.

## 4. How a ride works
1. You set the pickup and destination. The app prices the ride with the current fares (base, distance, time
   and, where they apply, time-of-day or demand surcharges and promotions).
2. A nearby driver accepts the ride. You can see their name, photo, vehicle, plate and rating.
3. **You pay the driver directly**, with the method you agree on (cash, pago móvil, Binance, Zelle or another
   one the app shows). **The amount is the one the app shows.** Kuulis never receives or holds that money.
4. At the end, each side rates the other.

When a promotion lowers the price, Kuulis covers the difference with the driver; you pay the discounted price.

## 5. Cancellations
You can cancel a ride before it starts. Frequent or abusive cancellations may lead to a suspension.

## 6. Conduct
Treat the driver with respect and follow the Community guidelines and traffic laws. Using Kuulis for illegal
activities, carrying forbidden items or putting others at risk is forbidden.

## 7. Safety
- Wear the helmet the driver offers you on motorbike rides.
- You can share your ride with a trusted contact and report any incident from the app.
- In an emergency, call emergency services first. Kuulis is not an emergency service.

## 8. Reports, suspension and closure

that puts other people at risk. We will tell you why unless the law prevents it.

- The app is provided "as is". It may have outages or bugs; we work to fix them but do not guarantee it will

- Items left behind are their owner's responsibility. We help you contact the driver.

## 10. Deleting your account

erase your personal data as the Privacy policy explains.
