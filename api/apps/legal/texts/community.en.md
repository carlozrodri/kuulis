These guidelines apply to riders and drivers on every ride. Breaking them may lead to a suspension or to the
account being closed.

## Respect
- Be courteous. Insults, threats, harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated.
- No physical contact or sexual comments. Respect personal space.
- Use the other person's details only for the ride. Do not contact them afterwards for personal reasons.

## Safety
- **Always a helmet** on motorbike rides: the driver carries one for the rider and the rider wears it.
- Drivers ride without alcohol or drugs, follow traffic laws and do not use the phone while driving except for
  navigation.
- One rider per motorbike ride, except a minor riding together as the law allows.
- Check that the person, plate and vehicle match the app before getting on.
- If something makes you feel unsafe, end the ride somewhere safe and report it.

## Forbidden items
Weapons, drugs, explosives, animals without prior agreement, anything that cannot be carried safely and
anything illegal.

## Payments
- The price is the one the app shows. No other amount is negotiated and no extra charges are added.
- Payment is made at the end of the ride, with the agreed method.

## Ratings and reports
- Rate honestly.
- Use reports for real problems. False reports are also a violation.

## Accounts
- One account per person. Do not lend or sell your account.
- The driver must be the person shown in the app, with the registered vehicle.
